01About

Curious by nature.
Deliberate by design.

I am Harshit Singh - a teenager from Haldwani, India, who thinks too much about AI, spends too much time building on the internet, and genuinely believes this is the most interesting time to be alive.

I explore the intersection of artificial intelligence, creativity, and digital building. I write books, make YouTube videos, build projects, and try to figure out how all of this fits together into something meaningful.

Not rigidly focused. Not trying to fit a mould. Just building toward something I cannot fully describe yet, and documenting the journey along the way.
